Privacy Policy

The short version: we don't sell anything, we don't run ads, and we don't track you. A few outside services only load if you say yes.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. What we collect

Nothing by default. This site has no accounts, no ads and no analytics trackers. We host our own fonts, so browsing the site doesn't send your information to font services.

  • Your privacy choice. When you click Accept or Decline, your browser remembers the answer on your device (in "local storage") so the banner doesn't keep popping up. It never leaves your device.
  • Messages you send us. If you use the contact form, your name, email and message go to the club's email (or a form service such as Formspree, if the club sets one up) so a coach can reply. We only use it to answer you.
  • Event sign-ups. Registration for events happens on outside forms such as Google Forms. Whatever you enter there is collected by the club through that service and used to run the event.

2. Outside services and your choice

Some features load content from other companies. Those companies may set cookies or collect data like your IP address. They only load if you click Accept:

  • Google Maps: the map on the Schedule page.
  • SugarWOD: the live workout feed on the WOD page, when it's connected.
  • Any future analytics or embeds the club adds will follow the same rule.

If you click Decline, those stay off and you'll see a button to load them one time if you want. Links to Instagram, the CrossFit Games or Google Forms only take you to those sites when you click them. Once you're there, their own privacy policies apply.

3. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

4. Students and minors

This site is for a high school club, so many visitors are students. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it. Photos of students are only posted with permission under CCSD rules, and we'll remove any photo on request.

5. How long we keep things

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up. Event registration info is kept for the event and its results, then cleaned up. Your privacy choice stays in your browser until you clear it or change it.

6. Your choices

  • Change your Accept/Decline choice any time with the button at the top of this page or the "Privacy settings" link in the footer.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information you've sent us, or to remove a photo.
  • Block or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or removal requests: reach us through the contact page.
