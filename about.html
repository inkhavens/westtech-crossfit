About us

The House of Hustle & Muscle

West Tech CrossFit is a non-profit CrossFit affiliate inside West Career & Technical Academy (WCTA) in Las Vegas. We train student athletes, coach adults and host competitions that bring the valley's CrossFit community to our gym.

Our story

A real CrossFit gym, right on campus

West Tech CrossFit started at WCTA in [YEAR — add founding year]. It grew out of a push across Clark County schools to bring CrossFit into PE and after-school programs, and it has become one of the best-known school programs in the Las Vegas CrossFit community.

Today we're an official, non-profit CrossFit affiliate. Students train here for free after school, adults train here too, and every year we open our doors for competitions that raise money for the program.
