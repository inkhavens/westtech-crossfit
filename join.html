Ask a coach
FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Do I need CrossFit experience?

Nope. Most of our athletes started with zero experience. Coaches teach every movement and scale every workout to your level.

Does it cost anything?

No. The club is free for WCTA students. There's no sign-up and no fee.

Do I have to come every day?

There are no attendance requirements, but consistency is how you get better. Aim for all three days when you can.

I'm not very strong yet. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every workout has Rx and scaled versions, and new athletes start light with a PVC pipe or empty bar until their form is solid.

Can I compete?

Yes. Club athletes compete in our Teen Fitness Series, local competitions and the CrossFit Open. Check the events page.

What should I wear?

Athletic shoes and clothes you can sweat in. Bring a water bottle. It's Las Vegas.
