Teen Fitness Series

A three-event competition series for teen athletes from CrossFit programs across the valley, hosted in the WCTA gymnasium. Each event has its own workout, a podium for every division, and athletes who compete in all three events land on the series leaderboard.

  • Divisions: Rx, Intermediate and Beginner (beginner is scaled to fit any athlete)
  • Heats every 20 minutes, with podium ceremonies after the final heat
  • Coaches: check with your athletes about the right division before they register

Fast Times at West Tech High

Our signature team competition and biggest fundraiser of the year. Every dollar goes back into West Tech CrossFit. Gyms from all over Las Vegas send teams to compete.

  • Teams of 3 (all male or all female) in Rx or Scaled divisions
  • Indoor and outdoor events, with the top teams battling in the final WOD
  • Full locker room access. No porta potties!
  • Bring your crew. Spectators welcome.

The CrossFit Open

Every year West Tech athletes join hundreds of thousands of people around the world in the CrossFit Open. It's three weeks of workouts that you do in our gym, judged by our coaches. Teens compete in the 14–15 and 16–17 age divisions, and every workout has Rx, scaled and foundations versions.

  • 2027 OpenDates to be announced by CrossFit
  • RegisterAt games.crossfit.com. Choose West Tech CrossFit as your affiliate.
